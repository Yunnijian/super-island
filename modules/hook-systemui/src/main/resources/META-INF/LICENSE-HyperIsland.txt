HyperIsland source notice
=========================

This module generates a restricted subset of source files from HyperIsland at build time.

The generated subset is modified deterministically during the build. The build removes
templates and integration points that this project does not use, including the AI and
download templates, notification filters, dispatcher fallback, and status-bar icon hook.
